# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want coolAI Gate to be a constructive, welcoming place for people interested in safe and useful AI systems. We commit to respectful, good-faith collaboration regardless of background, experience level, identity, or viewpoint.

## Expected behaviour

- Focus critique on ideas, code, and evidence — never on people.
- Assume good intent, ask clarifying questions, and welcome different perspectives.
- Share only information you are allowed to share.
- Help keep examples and discussions safe for real-world use.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats, discrimination, or hostile personal attacks.
- Publishing private, confidential, or personal information without permission.
- Deliberately submitting malicious code, secrets, or instructions intended to bypass safeguards.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that violate these standards. Repeated or serious violations may lead to a ban from participation.

To report a concern, contact the maintainer privately through the GitHub profile rather than opening a public issue. Reports will be handled discreetly and in good faith.
